Haven Adventurer’s Guide

Your goal is to protect Haven by following the story, growing stronger, and uncovering the source of the growing darkness.

Your next steps

  1. Open Quests and accept the current story chapter. Complete its objective, then return to claim gold, EXP, and merits. New chapters unlock in order.
  2. Use Adventure to fight enemies and earn rewards. Combat costs Energy; Attack deals damage, Defend reduces the next hit, and Skill deals a stronger strike.
  3. Visit Training to spend Energy on Strength, Defense, Speed, or Evasion. Strength and Defense help in fights; your level and stats also make Adventure enemies tougher.
  4. Buy gear and potions in the Market. Gear bonuses apply when bought. Find your gear and consumables in Inventory; use potions there to restore a resource.
  5. Enter the Dungeon when you are ready. Each floor has three enemies, costs Energy, and carries your health between fights. Clear floors in order to earn rewards and unlock the next floor.
  6. Take on the six Rank Quests for your level tier in the Quests journal for extra long-term challenges and rewards.

Helpful reminders

  • Health, Energy, and Glory regenerate over time. Rest fully restores Health and Energy, but has a long cooldown.
  • Check Home for your character’s stats and quick actions, and Stats for your lifetime progress.
  • If you’re unsure what to do next, follow the first available story quest and prepare for its objective.
